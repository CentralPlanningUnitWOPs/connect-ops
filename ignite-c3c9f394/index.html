02Connect People

From the first application to the week they are paid.

Documents arrive through the candidate's own portal and file themselves against their record. One record, end to end, nothing typed twice.

03Connect Compliance

Nobody reaches the rota unchecked.

Right to work and licence checks confirmed by a manager, dated and attributable. Licences checked again on a cycle by rule. The gate holds until all of it is green. When an audit lands, every record is already dated and signed, so producing it takes minutes rather than a scramble.

04Connect Payments

Their invoicing and accountancy, run by Connect.

Connect facilitates self billing invoicing and accountancy for your drivers. You confirm the week's rota is right, and pay is generated from it line by line.

05Connect Performance

Who needs a conversation first, and why.

The week's scorecards become a ranked roster. It finds who is sliding against their own form, not only who is low.
