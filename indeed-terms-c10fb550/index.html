Compliance review · Connect Ops recruitment front door · 26 Sep 2026

Does our Indeed automation break Indeed's terms?

The automated pipeline doesn't. Applicants apply in full on Indeed. Nothing we run logs into, scrapes or automates Indeed's website. Our replies take the route Indeed documents for employers: an email from the DSP's own recruitment inbox, answering Indeed's application email. Every message is about the job the person applied for, and the booking page books a session: it is not a second application. One smaller point should be tightened.

Taking applicants in Holds

A Gmail filter forwards Indeed's notification to Make, which sends Connect Ops five fields: name, relay address, subject, text and time. CVs are never copied.

Indeed rule ToS A.3.1ToS A.3.3ToS D.21
Why it holds

Indeed sends the application to the employer itself. It tells applicants the application may go on to the employer's chosen ATS or service provider. Reading your own inbox is not access to Indeed's site, so the ban on automated access doesn't apply.

This is not an Indeed partner integration. It should never be described as one.

The application Holds

Nothing happens until Indeed confirms a completed application. The booking link is kept out of the ad and sent only after the person has applied.

Indeed rule Indeed Apply PolicyMisleading content
Why it holds

Indeed forbids sending people elsewhere to apply, or asking them to apply again. The booking page asks for no CV, work history or account. It schedules a session that follows an application already complete on Indeed.

Messages to applicants Holds

One reply to Indeed's application email, then at most two reminders, at 24 and 72 hours. After 7 days nothing more is sent. Every reply leaves the DSP's own recruitment inbox and appears in the Indeed conversation under the employer's name. Indeed's own automatic message is switched off, so nobody hears twice.

Indeed rule Adding users (email access)MessagingToS D.6.4Email Policy
Why it holds

Indeed lets anyone on a job's application-updates list join a candidate's conversation by replying to the application email, with no Indeed account needed. That is exactly what these replies are. They also keep to Indeed's content rule: they cover only the job applied for, with no re-application request, no spam and no fishing for personal data.

Why this is not "automating Indeed" is set out below.

The booking page Holds

The page asks for name, mobile, email and how the applicant wants reminders. It also asks up to eight yes/no checks: licence held 12+ months, 6 points or fewer, right to work, and willingness to do a DBS. There is no fee, no account and no app.

Indeed rule User safety (PII)Jobseeker costsMisleading content
Why it holds

The page asks for nothing on Indeed's list of unnecessary personal data: no date of birth, NI number, bank details, ID documents, photo or home address. ID, right-to-work documents and bank details are collected only at onboarding, after the person has attended in person, which is outside Indeed.

Personal data Holds

A privacy notice names the DSP as controller. Reminders carry a stop link, one-click for booked applicants. Anyone who never attends is anonymised after 6 months without activity. There are no analytics or tracking pixels.

Indeed rule ToS B.1.1ToS B.5.4ToS B.6.3ToS A.6.3Indeed Apply Policy
Why it holds

Indeed makes the employer responsible for handling and keeping data lawfully, and for explaining what it collects. The data is used only for hiring and is never sold. Stop links take effect automatically, and no tracker is set that would need Indeed-side consent.

WhatsApp and text Holds

WhatsApp is offered only as a reminder channel the applicant opts into, and only for the session they booked. It is built but not yet switched on.

Indeed rule ToS D.21ToS Definitions
Why it holds

Indeed's definition of a text message includes WhatsApp, and texts are allowed when they relate to hiring for a job ad. A reminder the applicant asked for, about their own session, relates to hiring.

Automating our workflow isn't automating Indeed

Only two things are automated: when a reply goes out and how it is worded. Indeed receives an ordinary email reply from an address it has authorised for that conversation. The evidence:

  • The channel is Indeed's own. Indeed tells employers to add an email address under Edit job → Application updates. Anyone on that list gets the application emails. When they reply, the reply goes to the candidate's conversation email and joins the thread. Adding users Messaging
  • The sender is genuine and authorised. Replies come from the DSP's recruitment inbox, the address Indeed sends the applications to. Indeed requires a true sender address. It files each reply in Indeed Messages, where it can see, and if it chooses filter, every message. Nothing is hidden from Indeed. ToS B.1.5 ToS D.21
  • Indeed's website is never touched. Nothing logs in or uses a dashboard, an API or a browser. The rules on automated access and on automated systems using your account are about Indeed's own interfaces, and neither is engaged. ToS D.21 ToS D.7.2
  • Indeed sells the same thing. Indeed's tools fill job and candidate details into message templates and send automatic messages to new applicants. Its help pages also expect an employer's ATS or scheduling software to email candidates. What Indeed polices is content and consent, not whether a template was used. Messaging candidates ToS B.5.2 Adding users

What an objection would rest on. Indeed's definition of the "Site" includes its email messages, and D.21 bars submitting content "by automated means" other than Indeed's own tools. Indeed's help pages also point software to the candidate's email alias, not the conversation address. Neither forbids what we do, but that is where Indeed would start if it objected. These conditions keep us clear of it:

  • Replies only ever leave the inbox on the job's application-updates list, never a Connect Ops domain.
  • Each is a one-to-one reply to a real application.
  • There are no more than three per applicant.
  • Every message is about that job only.
  • Every stop request is honoured.

If Indeed still objected, the first message would move to Indeed's own Automations.

One point to tighten

Screening questions exist only on our page Tighten

The ads carry no Indeed screener questions, according to the August notes. So when an applicant answers "No", the process ends on our page rather than on Indeed.

Indeed rule Indeed Apply PolicyToS B.5.3
What to do

Indeed's policy is that screener questions in an outside flow must also be set up in Indeed Apply. Our page schedules rather than takes applications, but a knockout question is the nearest thing on it to an application step. Add the same four questions to each ad, so the booking page only confirms answers already given on Indeed.

Indeed can remove jobs or restrict accounts at its own discretion. Keeping within its terms lowers the risk but can't guarantee Indeed will agree. This is an operational review, not legal advice.
